Who We Are

At Myzora collection, we are more than just event planners — we are storytellers, experience crafters, and curators of timeless memories.

Rooted in the heart of the hospitality and events industry, our focus is on delivering bespoke, premium events that reflect the unique personality, style, and vision of every client.

We specialize in creating unforgettable celebrations, including weddings, landmark birthdays, anniversaries, engagements, proposals, and corporate events. From planning to curated rentals, every detail is handled with care, creativity, and intentionality.

Our mission is clear: to transform your dreams into experiences that feel effortless yet extraordinary — events that linger in the hearts of those who attend.

Myzora collection is here to make your milestones memorable, whether now or in the future. We don't just plan events — we craft timeless experiences.
