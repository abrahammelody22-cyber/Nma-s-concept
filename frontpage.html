Founder's Story

Abraham Melody Akachukwunaemenma

Founder & Creative Director of Myzora collection

As a certified event planner, organizing events has always come naturally to me. My passion began early. While growing up in a close-knit community, I took the initiative to open a small local school for children during school breaks. At the end of each session, I would organize beautifully executed graduation parties for the children and their parents, long before I even knew what an event planner was.

The professional journey officially began in 2018 when I coordinated my sister's wedding. A guest recognized my innate talent for organizing and encouraged me to turn it into a business. That moment sparked the idea that led to extensive research, my very first flyer, and ultimately, the launch of MyRubiees Events.

Building this brand has not been without its challenges — from navigating early partnerships to overcoming the hurdles of establishing a new business. However, every experience has sharpened my vision: to curate premium, well-structured events that create lasting memories.

Today, with the support of a dedicated team and a relentless drive for growth, I continue to invest heavily in learning from industry experts. My mission is to build a premium, full-service events brand that not only meets but exceeds client expectations — and ultimately, to build a legacy that outlives me.

“At Myzora collection, we lead with passion, creativity, and a commitment to excellence — turning moments into memories.”
